✿ Our story ✿

Grown by women,
wrapped like a gift

FLWRPWR is a women-owned flower company with one rule: good vibes, great flower.

We think buying flower should feel like walking into your favorite boutique, soft colors, pretty shelves, zero intimidation. So that is what we are building: a flower shop that feels like a gift store, stocked with our own 4g Smalls.

The first drop is Thai Stick, Northern Lights and Maui Wowie. The wholesale sheet already names the next three, Acapulco Gold, Trainwreck and Afghani, still growing, worth the wait.

On Instagram we keep it plain: take a break, unplug, unwind. New month, new beginnings. That is the whole voice, the feed is where launch day gets announced first, so follow along at @flwrpwr_co.
