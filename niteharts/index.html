Parking plan

Closest · On site

Snapdragon Stadium

Niteharts sells official on-site parking. Buy ahead through the festival site. A fixed public price is not posted, so use the checkout total rather than a resale estimate.

Paid overflow · Verify event access

Mission City Garage

Mission City Corporate Center Garage at Friars Road and Northside Drive is the stadium’s named paid overflow option. Event-day access and price are not posted for Niteharts.

For park-and-Uber, use the designated drop-off on northbound Mission Village Drive between Friars Road and Jacaranda Street.

Free park + ride · Green Line

Old Town

Old Town Transit Center, 4009 Taylor St. · 412 spaces. Ride the Green Line directly to Stadium Station. Station parking is free and limited to 24 hours.

Free park + ride · Green Line

Morena / Grantville

Morena/Linda Vista, 5198 Friars Rd. · 199 spaces. Union Grantville, 4510 Alvarado Canyon Rd. · 100 spaces. Both connect to Stadium Station on the Green Line; station parking is limited to 24 hours.

Do not use retail lots unless the property explicitly allows event parking. Nearby shopping-center parking can be customer-only or tow-enforced. Unposted event rates stay TBA.

Prohibited items

Leave these at home or in the car. All items are subject to search before entry.

  • Aerosol products or cans
  • Amplified music, bullhorns, air horns or speakers
  • Audio or video recording devices
  • Bags larger than 12 × 6 × 12 in.
  • Banners and signs
  • Bats and sticks, including signs with sticks
  • Beach balls or other inflatables
  • Beverages, including alcohol
  • Bicycles
  • Blankets
  • Bota bags
  • Bottles, cans or containers, empty or full
  • Bubble-making devices
  • Costume masks
  • Costumes resembling police, fire, security or medical uniforms
  • Drones or other motorized devices
  • Illegal drugs or paraphernalia, including medical marijuana
  • E-liquid or e-juice
  • Metal bottles or water bottles larger than 1L
  • Eye drops
  • Firearms
  • Fireworks or incendiary devices
  • Folding chairs, stools or lawn furniture
  • Sporting equipment
  • Gas masks
  • Glow sticks
  • GoPro cameras
  • Helium balloons
  • Ice chests or coolers
  • Jewelry or clothing with spikes over 0.5 in.
  • Knives
  • Laser devices or pointers
  • LED gloves or microlights for light shows
  • LED poi or orbitals
  • Liquid makeup
  • Mace or pepper spray
  • Markers, pens or spray paint
  • Massagers
  • Noisemakers or whistles
  • Offensive clothing that demeans or promotes violence against a protected group
  • Outside food, beverages or candy
  • Pacifiers
  • Pets, except service animals
  • Professional photo, video or audio equipment
  • Segways or hoverboards
  • Selfie sticks
  • Skateboards, scooters or rollerblades
  • Smoking or nicotine products, including vapes
  • Stickers or flyers
  • Stuffed animals or dolls
  • Tents or shade structures
  • Totem poles, telescopic poles or pool noodles
  • Toy guns, water guns or slingshots
  • Umbrellas
  • Unsealed over-the-counter medication
  • Unsealed gum, mints or breath strips
  • Unsealed tampons or pads at entry
  • Vapor sticks
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Wind chairs or sofas

Allowed exceptions: one clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 in. or one purse/clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 in.; one empty clear plastic or rubber water bottle up to 1L; strollers stored under the ticketed seat or in a designated stroller area.
