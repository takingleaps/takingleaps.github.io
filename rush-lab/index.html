How a Rush Lab drop works

1
Pick a hero, not a catalog

Every batch leads with one glasses pair for ads. Streetwear rides along as add-ons and bundles.

2
Source on AliExpress, not Temu packaging

Temu was the inspo, AliExpress is the supplier path — so orders don’t arrive in orange Temu bags under a Rush label.

3
Be upfront on delivery

Listings showed 5–7 day fastest delivery. Put the real window on the product page. Surprise shipping is what kills repeat buyers.

4
Kill fast, scale the winner

If it doesn’t sell in the first test, it’s out. Lab means experiments — Batch 02 is already waiting below.

Why only one-size stuff?

Batch 01 avoids tees, hoodies, and sized fits on purpose. Caps adjust, chains and glasses are one-size, so your return rate stays low while you’re still learning ads.

Is this a checkout?

Not yet — this page is the storefront concept with the real supplier links. Use “View on AliExpress” to verify the live listing, price, and stock before you list it in Shopify or any store builder.

What about the name Rush?

Plain “Rush” for sunglasses is crowded (Julbo Rush, Wiley X Rush, Nike Show X Rush). “Rush Lab” is the distinct version — and it still works when you expand past glasses.

What should I do next?

Order samples of the two glasses first, shoot them on-face, then set the store’s shipping page from the real delivery you experience — not just the listing’s fastest estimate.
